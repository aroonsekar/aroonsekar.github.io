Hello,

01 Jun 2026 · 2 min read

I've had this website for a while now. It started as a thing I threw together to have something to point people at — a tic-tac-toe game, a timeline of my life, my resume, my works.

But I've been meaning to write more. Not in the LinkedIn-post, "excited-to-announce" sense. Just actual writing. Things I'm figuring out, things I built and what broke, ideas that feel half-baked but worth putting down somewhere.

So this is that place now. No comments, no analytics, no algorithm to please. Just a blog like old times — words on a page, on a site I own, updated whenever something's worth saying.

I build mobile apps for a living now. I mess around with quant finance models on weekends. I once simulated a city flood in Unreal Engine for a water utility company. There's probably something worth writing about in all of that.
